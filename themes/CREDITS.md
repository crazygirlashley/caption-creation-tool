# Theme artwork credits

Background images shipped with the built-in themes. Every source image is
in the public domain or dedicated to it (CC0 1.0), so they can be used,
changed and redistributed freely; credit is given here anyway.

## Bimbo — `Bimbo_kisses.gif`

- Source: "Lipstick stain png sticker, transparent", rawpixel —
  <https://www.rawpixel.com/image/7133143/png-sticker-public-domain>
- License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain —
  <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Changes: the kiss print was cut out of its preview background, recolored
  (reds, hot pinks, berry), scattered densely at varied sizes and angles into
  a seamless repeating tile on the theme's pink, and lightly animated (a soft
  pulse).

## Hypno — `Hypno_spiral.gif`

- Source: "MagicSpiral.gif" by Edo 555, Wikimedia Commons —
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:MagicSpiral.gif>
- License: public domain (released by the author)
- Changes: cropped to the spiral, enlarged, recolored to violet and electric
  teal, darkened toward the edges.

## Latex — `Latex_gloss.jpg`

- Source: "Black leather texture background", rawpixel (via Openverse) —
  <https://www.rawpixel.com/> (free image, CC0)
- License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain —
  <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Changes: the folds were isolated and contrast-boosted into a wet-look
  sheen, tinted cherry red on black, and made into a seamless tile.

## Pink Pill — `Pink_Pill_pills.gif`

- Source: "Pill (NIH BioArt 407)" capsule illustrations, NIH BioArt Source
  (US National Institutes of Health), via Wikimedia Commons —
  <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Pill_(NIH_BioArt_407_-_633323).png>
  and the 633308 / 633318 variants
- License: public domain (work of the US federal government)
- Changes: recolored (hot pink, pastel pink, medical teal), scattered at
  varied sizes and angles into a seamless tile on pink, lightly animated
  (a gentle wobble).
