# Theme artwork credits

Background images shipped with the built-in themes. Both source images are
dedicated to the public domain (CC0 1.0), so they can be used, changed and
redistributed freely; credit is given here anyway.

## Bimbo — `Bimbo_kisses.gif`

- Source: "Lipstick stain png sticker, transparent", rawpixel —
  <https://www.rawpixel.com/image/7133143/png-sticker-public-domain>
- License: CC0 1.0 Public Domain —
  <https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/>
- Changes: the kiss print was cut out of its preview background, recolored
  (red, hot pink, berry), arranged into a repeating tile on the theme's pink,
  and lightly animated (a soft pulse).
